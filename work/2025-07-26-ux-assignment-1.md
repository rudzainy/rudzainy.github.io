<h2 id="opening-statement">Opening Statement</h2>
<p>Sometime in July 2022, I got an interview invitation for a UIUX Designer position. After the first Zoom interview, I was given an assignment to be completed within a few days.</p>
<p>The assignment was to re-design and build a WordPress mockups for two websites.</p>
<div class="post-section-head" aria-hidden="true">
	<span class="head-tab"><i class="icon-workflow"></i><span>02 &middot; design, then code</span></span>
</div>
<h2 id="unpacking-the-problem">Unpacking the Problem</h2>
<p>I broke down the process into two parts – design and code.</p>
<h3 id="design">Design</h3>
<p>I didn’t have much time to work with, therefore I chose to not focus on UI too much, as both websites have a distinct branding theme. I focused instead on solving UX problems such as information hierarchy and basic UIUX housecleaning.</p>
<h3 id="code">Code</h3>
<p>I had to re-build the current theme and faced some challanges getting myself accustomed to how the WordPress theme author manages their codebase. I managed to convert the design from Figma into code utilizing plugins in Figma to optimize my process flow.</p>
