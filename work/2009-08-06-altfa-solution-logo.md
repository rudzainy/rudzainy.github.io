<p>A colleague of mine asked me to design a logo for his personal web development solutions company.</p>
<p>This design was rejected, however I have always liked the symbolism behind the combination of asterisk (*) and the letter A.</p>
<p>I wanted to illustrate the company’s problem solving methodology which considers multiple solutions to a problem and always proceed to selecting the best fit for clients (the asterisk is divided into three arrows pointing inwards).</p>
