<p>It's very easy to say yes <br>
 and take actions for something that,  <br>
 in your heart,  <br>
 you truly believe is right. <br>
There’s zero doubt. <br>
No regrets.</p>
<p>It’s not so easy though  <br>
 to say yes  <br>
 and choose the lesser evil  <br>
 between two wrongs.  <br>
However,   <br>
 deep down  <br>
 you believe that actions  <br>
 need to be taken. <br>
You dip your hands  <br>
 into the gutter.</p>
<p>It has to be done. <br>
You take  <br>
 every  <br>
 bit  <br>
 of responsibility  <br>
 for your actions.</p>
<p>It’s even harder to stay silent. <br>
Because you know that you will never be able to live with yourself  <br>
 if you say yes  <br>
 to a wrong.  <br>
You understand  <br>
 that you may never find peace. <br>
But you endure,  <br>
 keeping everything inside until one day,  <br>
 hopefully,  <br>
 your silence is heard.</p>
<p>Ultimately,  <br>
 the decision is yours alone to make.  <br>
 Do what you believe in your heart is right. <br>
When you do,  <br>
 God willing,  <br>
 it will be the right decision.</p>
	</article>
