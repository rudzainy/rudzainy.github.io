An unsolicited concept redesign of Malaysia's biggest forum, collapsing nine categories into three lanes and putting moderation in the open.

# About

“Rombak LYN” is a self-directed concept study. Not affiliated with Lowyat.NET, and no original screens are reproduced. The brief I set myself was to fix mobile, fix navigation and density, and make moderation and thread quality visible, without stripping the density the regulars stayed for.

Seven hi-fi screens are drawn as live HTML inside the post: forum index (desktop), index and thread list (mobile), thread view and reply sheet, search, and a Trade Zone listing with the seller's trust record.
