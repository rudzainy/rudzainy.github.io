## The market on the tracks 🚂

Some markets you walk through. This one, you share with a train. Vendors lay their stalls right along the rails, and when the horn sounds, the awnings fold back, the baskets pull in, the train slides past inches from the produce — and then everything unfolds again as if nothing happened.
