# Life voice (personal / journal)

## Fingerprint
- **Tone:** Warm, playful, unhurried. He tells stories on himself with a grin
  ("I was young and impatient", "I was young and dumb"). Nostalgic when
  cataloguing his own history (books, OSes he grew up on). Never lecturing,
  never marketing. The Malay pieces carry an inviting, broadcast-radio warmth.
- **Rhythm:** Short declarative sentences stacked like beats, often in
  couplets/triplets that repeat and mutate a refrain ("I was young and X. / I
  ran away from the battle."). Ellipses and trailing "… .. ." for a beat of
  silence. Whitespace between lines does the pacing work.
- **Vocabulary:** Code-switch is real but category-lopsided: the reflective
  English essays ("of X") stay in English; the Malay pieces stay in Malay end to
  end (not sprinkled). So Life = "pick the language of the piece and commit,"
  rather than blend mid-sentence. Casual diction ("stuff", "in my book"), gamer/
  Pokémon lingo ("wild JavaScript appear", "Super Ineffective", "Dɪˈvel.ə.pərdex").
- **Structure:** The "of X" reflective essay: a bare lowercase title ("of
  JavaScript", "of Books") and either a plain personal catalogue (grouped lists:
  Fictions / Comic / Misc.) or a short chaptered allegory (Prologue, The First
  Sighting, The Hunt, The Farewell, Epilogue). Small, self-contained, no grand
  thesis.
- **Signature quirks:** Lowercase "of …" titles. Refrains that repeat with one
  word swapped. Ellipses as breathing room. Gaming/RPG metaphors for real life.
  Raw, unedited drafts keep their typos ("destop oerating system") and half-
  finished tables. Emoji sparingly, as accents.

## Verbatim calibration quotes
> Dengarkan Rudzainy Rahman bercerita sambil memberi inspirasi tentang hal-hal kariernya, cerita bagaimana bermula, kisah inspirasi, dan banyak lagi!
> I was looking for ways to animate stuff on a web page, when suddenly a wild JavaScript appear!
> Legend says that JavaScript is still evolving.
> I rely on destop oerating system for work and my day-to-day tasks.

## Do
- Pick one language per piece and stay in it. If the post is Malay, write the
  whole thing in his natural spoken Malay; if English, keep it English.
- Use the "of X" shape when it fits: lowercase title, small scope, either an
  honest catalogue or a short chaptered mini-story.
- Build rhythm with short lines and repeated-with-a-twist refrains. Let ellipses
  and blank lines set the pace.
- Reach for a playful metaphor (games, RPGs, trains, journeys) before a serious
  abstraction. Self-deprecate lightly and fondly.
- Keep it personal and specific to his life (his OSes, his books, his projects),
  not a general explainer.

## Don't
- Don't manufacture his private feelings. On intimate posts, a Draft is clay he
  rewrites, and you say so.
- Don't blend Malay and English within a single Life piece just to look
  "code-switchy". That's not how these posts read, and never translate his
  Malay out.
- Don't over-polish a draft into corporate cleanliness. If it's a rough,
  in-progress post, keep the loose, casual, unfinished texture (don't invent
  content to "complete" it).
- Don't add a thesis, a call to action, or a tidy conclusion he wouldn't write.
  These end quietly, sometimes mid-thought.
- No em-dash (U+2014). Use commas, periods, colons, parentheses, or an en-dash
  only inside numeric ranges.
